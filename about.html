Prompts I actually used

The prompting style that worked best across the three months. Plain-language symptoms, one change at a time, willing to revert.

  1. "On mobile the first scroll has resistance. The second one feels normal. Desktop is fine. Don't change desktop."
  2. "The shocked Brady transition goes in and out and feels like a struggle. It used to feel good in the old file."
  3. "Yes, do this — let's test one at a time."
  4. "Desktop sees 2 votes. Mobile sees 10. Same database."
  5. "These are not two separate sections. The animation we built is replacing the existing one."
  6. "I voted for Alexander on the live site and it didn't add one but it knew it was my vote."
  7. "Make the comments dev-friendly. The point of everything is that this is animation design inspo for devs and non-technical people."

What these have in common: a specific symptom, a contrast (where it works vs. doesn't), and a constraint (what not to break). Almost no technical jargon. Claude diagnosed the cause; I held the taste line.
